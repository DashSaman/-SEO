# Publisher / News SEO Playbook

## Core jobs

Publishers need reliable crawl/discovery, clear article identity, freshness accuracy, strong topic architecture, media eligibility and editorial trust. Speed of publication does not excuse low-quality/scaled content.

## Architecture

`Home → Sections/topics → Articles → Author/topic archives`

Avoid uncontrolled tag archives and duplicate category/tag paths. Keep important evergreen explainers separate from fast news updates when intent differs.

## Article requirements

- descriptive headline/title;
- clear publication and modified dates only when real;
- author/byline and appropriate profile;
- sources/quotes and corrections where relevant;
- primary image/media crawlable;
- Article/NewsArticle structured data when accurate;
- canonical stable URL;
- related contextual links;
- no intrusive interstitial that destroys usability.

## Freshness

Do not change timestamps merely to appear fresh. Update when material facts change, describe meaningful revisions where high-stakes, and maintain evergreen URLs for durable explainers.

## News / Discover / AI

Use current Google News/Discover/Search documentation rather than myths about “submission.” Strong images, clear titles, trustworthy reporting and topic relevance help eligibility; no surface guarantees traffic. AI-generated summaries/assistive writing still require editorial verification and source accuracy.

## Crawl speed

For high-volume publishers:
- clean current news sitemaps where applicable;
- accurate `lastmod`;
- fast stable origin/CDN;
- limit low-value archives/parameters;
- monitor Googlebot logs and Page Indexing;
- avoid 5xx/429 during breaking-news spikes.

## Paywalls

Use supported paywall/subscription structured-data/markup patterns where applicable and ensure crawlers/users receive policy-compliant equivalent content, not cloaking.

## UGC/comments

Moderate spam, links and abuse. Keep valuable comments if they add evidence/community context; do not let comment pages create infinite crawl spaces.

## KPI stack

- qualified search/news/discover clicks;
- returning users/subscriptions;
- article/section indexation latency;
- recrawl latency for breaking updates;
- topic authority/evergreen traffic;
- media/video visibility;
- AI citations/referrals;
- correction/factual-quality metrics.

## PASS gate

PASS when dates/bylines/sources are truthful, architecture is finite, important articles are discovered quickly, corrections/editorial ownership are clear, media is eligible, and performance is measured by audience/subscription value rather than article volume.
