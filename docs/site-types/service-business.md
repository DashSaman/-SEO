# Service Business SEO Playbook

## Goal

Rank for qualified service/problem demand and convert it into legitimate calls, forms, bookings or consultations.

## Architecture

`Home → Service hubs → Individual services → Industries/use cases → Locations (only when real) → Case studies/resources/contact`

## Service page requirements

Show:
- exact service scope;
- who it is for/not for;
- process/deliverables;
- location/availability;
- pricing model/range when feasible;
- timelines/requirements;
- proof: case studies, credentials, team, examples;
- limitations;
- FAQs based on real sales/support questions;
- clear contact/quote/booking CTA.

Avoid generic pages where only service keyword is swapped.

## Intent mapping

Separate:
- commercial service queries;
- emergency/immediate queries;
- comparison/cost queries;
- informational troubleshooting;
- local/location queries.

One page should own a coherent intent cluster; avoid synonym cannibalization.

## Trust

For high-consideration services provide real business identity, address/service area, phone, licenses/insurance/accreditations where applicable, team expertise, privacy/terms, authentic reviews and transparent case studies.

## Local layer

If service is geographically delivered, combine website service pages with accurate Google Business Profile/location evidence. Do not create fake offices or city doorway pages.

## Authority

Strong assets:
- project/case-study evidence;
- before/after methodology;
- calculators/estimators;
- technical guides;
- original local/industry data;
- professional association/partner references.

## Measurement

- qualified non-brand service clicks;
- phone/form/booked conversion;
- lead quality and revenue;
- query→service-page mapping;
- local pack/organic visibility by area;
- assisted guide→service conversions;
- branded demand/reviews;
- AI referrals/citations where material.

## PASS gate

PASS when each priority service page matches real commercial intent, contains business-specific proof, local pages are genuine, technical eligibility is clean and success is tied to qualified leads/revenue rather than impressions alone.
