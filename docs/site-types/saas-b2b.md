# SaaS / B2B SEO Playbook

**Goal:** generate qualified pipeline, not traffic for its own sake.

## Architecture

Recommended layers:

`Home → Product/Platform → Solutions/Use cases → Integrations → Industry pages → Comparison/alternatives → Resources/docs/case studies`

Only create use-case/industry/integration pages when the product truly supports the scenario and page has distinct evidence.

## Query portfolio

Prioritize:

1. category/problem queries;
2. high-intent feature/use-case queries;
3. integration queries;
4. industry-specific needs;
5. comparison/alternative queries;
6. implementation/tutorial queries;
7. thought leadership/original data.

Avoid mass “X for every industry/city” templates without real differentiation.

## Commercial page evidence

Strong pages show:

- what product does;
- who it is for/not for;
- concrete workflows;
- integrations/requirements;
- pricing or buying process when possible;
- screenshots/demo/video;
- security/compliance facts;
- case studies/customer proof with permission;
- limitations/tradeoffs;
- clear demo/trial/contact action.

## Comparison pages

Use verifiable feature/pricing data and a stated update date. Do not fabricate competitor weaknesses or fake “reviews.” Prefer fair fit-based comparisons.

## Integration pages

Require actual integration support, setup steps, authentication/version requirements, screenshots/examples and limitations. Thin logo-directory pages should not become a pSEO index farm.

## Authority strategy

Best B2B assets often include:

- benchmark reports;
- original datasets;
- open-source tools;
- calculators/templates;
- engineering/security documentation;
- customer case studies;
- expert research and industry commentary.

Use these for Digital PR and genuine partner references.

## Technical priorities

- keep marketing pages/indexable while app/account areas are properly controlled;
- avoid JS shell failures on product pages;
- manage docs/version URLs/canonicals;
- integrate docs/blog/product architecture through useful internal links;
- keep old integrations/features from becoming stale 200 pages;
- monitor migrations/rebrands carefully.

## AI Search

Make product facts, compatibility, pricing/process, limitations and terminology explicit and current. Allow intended search crawlers; separate training controls. Measure ChatGPT referrals, Bing AI citations and Google AI reporting where available.

## KPI stack

- qualified non-brand clicks;
- demo/trial/contact conversions;
- pipeline/revenue influenced;
- target account engagement;
- branded search growth;
- product/category visibility;
- relevant referring domains;
- AI referrals/citations;
- content-assisted opportunities.

## PASS gate

PASS when priority pages match commercial intent, contain product-specific proof, no mass thin solution/integration pages exist, technical eligibility is clean, and search traffic is joined to CRM-quality outcomes.
