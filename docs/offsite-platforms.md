# Offsite Platforms — Search, Entity & AI Evidence Without Spam

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-26  
**Goal:** understand where genuine third-party evidence can improve discoverability/reputation and where manipulation creates risk.

## Core rule

Presence on a high-authority platform is valuable only when it serves that platform’s users and accurately represents the entity. Do not create fake consensus, reviews, answers or mentions to “train” search engines/LLMs.

## Reddit

Potential value:

- authentic customer/community discussion;
- discovery of real questions/pain points/language;
- third-party product experiences;
- support/feedback when a brand transparently participates.

Do:

- identify real recurring questions;
- answer transparently when affiliation exists;
- improve product/docs from feedback;
- measure referral and brand-search effects.

Do not:

- operate sockpuppets;
- buy/upvote fake recommendations;
- seed fabricated comparisons;
- mass-drop links.

No first-party Search/AI provider documentation establishes fake Reddit mentions as a ranking/citation factor.

## YouTube

Potential value:

- tutorial/product demonstrations;
- independent reviews;
- visual evidence and expert explanation;
- Google video/search surfaces.

For owned video:

- descriptive title/description;
- accurate captions/transcript;
- strong thumbnail and watch-page context;
- chapters where useful;
- link to canonical supporting resources;
- embed on a relevant indexable page when it improves the page.

Search benefit is content/surface-dependent; do not assume YouTube links pass a special universal authority score.

## Wikipedia / Wikidata

Potential value: independent entity/reference data when the entity genuinely meets project policies.

Rules:

- notability must be supported independently;
- do not use as a self-promotional directory;
- do not manipulate editors or references;
- do not create fake coverage to manufacture eligibility.

Neither Wikipedia nor Wikidata is required for Google/ChatGPT visibility.

## GitHub

Potential value:

- real open-source software;
- technical documentation;
- reproducible datasets/benchmarks;
- issue/discussion evidence;
- developer entity/reputation.

Good practice:

- complete README/docs/license;
- releases/changelog;
- security/contact policy for serious tools;
- canonical project/organization links;
- cite reproducible methodology/data.

Do not create empty repositories solely for backlinks/keywords.

## Stack Overflow / technical Q&A

Potential value:

- genuine technical answers;
- recurring implementation problems;
- product documentation gaps.

Rules:

- answer the question completely;
- disclose affiliation where required/appropriate;
- link only when it materially supports the answer;
- avoid coordinated promotional posting.

## Industry publications

Potential value:

- earned editorial coverage;
- expert commentary;
- original research citations;
- market legitimacy.

Priority should be niche relevance and readership, not only third-party “domain authority.” Paid advertorials need appropriate disclosure/link qualification.

## Review platforms

Potential value:

- customer decision support;
- local prominence/reputation;
- third-party evidence for brand perception.

Rules:

- ask for honest reviews without gating;
- no fake/incentivized reviews that violate platform policy;
- respond to issues;
- track themes, not just star average.

## Marketplaces / app stores

Potential value:

- product discovery;
- trusted transaction/review layer;
- category/entity data;
- high-intent branded visibility.

Optimize the actual listing: accurate title/category, specifications, media, availability/pricing and support. Do not clone marketplace copy to owned pages without added first-party value.

## Business directories

Useful when:

- platform is authoritative/relevant to customers;
- listing corresponds to a real business/location;
- NAP/category/contact facts are current.

Low value/risk when:

- thousands of irrelevant auto-submission directories;
- keyword-stuffed business names;
- duplicate/fake locations;
- listings exist solely for followed links.

## Forums / niche communities

These can reveal language, objections and emerging topics that keyword tools miss. Use them for research and genuine participation. A community mention is not inherently a ranking signal or AI citation guarantee.

## Offsite evidence scoring framework

Score opportunities on:

1. **Audience relevance** — do target users actually use it?
2. **Editorial independence** — is evidence genuinely third-party?
3. **Entity accuracy** — does it clearly identify the correct brand/person/product?
4. **Evidence depth** — review, test, quote, dataset or actual experience?
5. **Freshness** — current enough for the topic?
6. **Referral potential** — can it send qualified users?
7. **Policy safety** — no fake/paid-followed manipulation?
8. **Maintainability** — can incorrect/stale data be corrected?

A low-relevance high-DA page can be less strategically useful than a smaller authoritative niche community.

## AI citation research use

When monitoring ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity/Copilot/Google AI responses, record cited domains by **source type**:

- owned brand;
- official/government;
- media/industry publication;
- review site;
- forum/community;
- video/social;
- marketplace;
- encyclopedia/reference;
- competitor.

Then ask: what legitimate evidence is missing from our ecosystem? Do not respond by fabricating the same source type.

## Measurement

Track separately:

- new mentions;
- linked vs unlinked mentions;
- referral sessions;
- assisted conversions;
- branded search demand;
- review volume/sentiment;
- citation share in a controlled prompt/query set;
- earned referring domains;
- support/lead quality.

Do not combine all into one invented “authority score.”

## PASS / FAIL

PASS when offsite work creates real useful information/participation, affiliation is transparent, evidence is authentic, platform policies are respected, and outcomes are measured beyond links.

FAIL when success depends on fake personas, fake reviews, mass links, purchased undisclosed editorial coverage or manipulated community consensus.
