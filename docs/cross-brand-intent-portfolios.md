## Transferable procedure

The following is `INFERENCE` from this bounded implementation, not a search-engine ranking rule:

1. Inventory existing public URLs before proposing routes. Preserve title/H1/canonical observations and an observation date; exclude private account, customer and unpublished content. Store raw operational evidence outside public Git.
2. Assign ownership by the reader's task. Keep a frozen specialist brand's commercial routes intact and improve another brand's existing commercial pages before creating synonyms. Editorial responsibility does not establish legal ownership, partnership or a `sameAs` relationship.
3. Give each candidate a stable ID, reader job, intent, owner, priority, destination, action, source IDs, research status, market evidence, publication state and explicit limit. Separate editorial judgment from measured opportunity.
4. Group related jobs at one useful destination when appropriate. Count jobs, existing destinations, proposed groups, drafted articles and published articles separately. Do not turn a minimum topic count into a minimum URL count.
5. Test the grouping semantically as well as mechanically. In this case, one-way loss methodology and privacy-aware packet evidence were moved away from the MTU calculator because they answer different jobs. Exact normalized-title uniqueness alone cannot establish distinct intent.
6. Prefer a useful improvement to an existing page. The first editorial unit is the existing RouterOS-Winbox lesson's Backup/Export/Safe Mode material. The already expanded voice-network service should not be padded simply because five inventory jobs map to it.
7. Keep reciprocal referral proposals as documents until that site's own change process permits them. TehNet's MyTel proposals were not deployed and were not represented as edits in the MyTel repository.
8. Gate publication on applicable primary-source review, technical version/lab qualification, editorial differentiation, valid links and a relevant CTA. A candidate inventory is an input to that gate, not its substitute.

## Reproducibility and boundaries

The public [stdlib validator](https://github.com/DashSaman/TehNet/blob/87805ecc4d2306996f2fdfac50c893e440574f92/tools/validate_intent_portfolio_20261101.py) performs 20 artifact consistency checks without credential or network. Default execution is read-only; `--write-receipt` is an explicit receipt rewrite. It checks the stored inventory, not a fresh live crawl. The receipt records six LF-normalized source digests and the validator digest. A later content change needs a new dated observation rather than silently relabeling this snapshot.

Checks include source references, candidate states, destination membership, frozen MyTel titles/H1s and document-only proposals. They do not prove semantic completeness, article accuracy, source freshness, current availability, cannibalization, index eligibility or commercial value. TehNet's deliberate noindex remains a separate owner gate; noindex was not changed by this package. Source/private-value checks protect publication evidence but do not settle credential rotation, remote history or Origin security.
